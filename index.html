01 / THE CREATIVE BEHIND THE WORK

A little about me.

AM7.CREATIVITY WITH A LITTLE EXTRA SPARK ✦

I’m a Creative Digital Media Professional with hands-on experience in social media management, graphic design and video editing.

My work has included creating social media content, designing brochures and product catalogues, producing marketing graphics, and editing both short-form and long-form videos. I enjoy bringing ideas to life through strong visuals, clear storytelling and thoughtful execution.
